Opening priorities

  1. Sign in to the competition services and record your assigned team number privately.
  2. Access every assigned VM and confirm the starting state.
  3. Change the required scoring credentials immediately.
  4. Submit the corresponding PCRs in Quotient.
  5. Verify authentication and scored services through the assigned public addresses.
  6. Record the baseline: hosts, services, listeners, users, routes, firewall/NAT, and logs.
  7. Begin defensive cleanup and hardening while continuously checking service functionality.
  8. Monitor Quotient for injects and deadlines throughout the event.

Rules that affect operations

  • Do not remove any required account. The named accounts are used by scoring checks.
  • Changing credentials requires a Password Change Request (PCR).
  • Keep services on their assigned public IPs and preserve the competition addressing.
  • Do not block source IPs or create rules that only permit the scoring engine.
  • Do not alter a service’s scoring behavior or replace expected content with a shortcut.
  • Do not disable forwarding to the Black Team-controlled Splunk indexer.
  • Do not scan, attack, or access another team’s infrastructure.
  • Use only public, open-source information and avoid paid or account-gated resources during the event.
  • AI tools may assist with system administration, but may not write or edit any part of an inject response.
  • Do not include your personal name on injects; use the assigned team number.

Inject submission requirements

  • Submit through Quotient before the displayed deadline.
  • Submit a response to every inject requiring a written deliverable, even if incomplete.
  • Use PDF format and the filename pattern team##_inject##.pdf, unless the inject says otherwise.
  • Use the team number, not a personal name.
  • Label and center screenshots, label tables, use consistent formatting, and write for a non-technical audience.
