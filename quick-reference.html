Competition quick reference

Public summary of the packet. Use the official packet and portal announcements as authoritative.

First 30 minutes

Suggested pacing, not a competition rule. Follow official packet instructions and portal announcements first; adjust for access delays.

  1. 0-5 min | Change and record passwords

  2. 5-10 min | Map the topology

  3. 10-15 min | Confirm host access

  4. 15-20 min | Verify scored services

  5. 20-25 min | Capture a read-only baseline

  6. 25-30 min | Harden carefully and recheck

Required services

Service What to preserve
HTTP/HTTPS Availability and expected page content.
SSH Reachability and valid account authentication.
FTP Reachability, authentication, and inject-specified behavior.
AD/DNS Directory authentication, name resolution, and dependencies.
POP3 Reachability and expected account/service behavior.
RDP Where present, valid account login and host availability.
